Billing FAQ

Refunds

Can I get a refund for my monthly subscription?

Monthly subscription payments are non-refundable. This includes partial months after a plan change.

Are annual subscriptions refundable?

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full if cancelled within 14 days of the initial purchase.

Cancellation

What happens when I cancel my subscription?

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Your workspace stays active until the end of the current billing period, and you are not charged again.

How do I cancel?

  1. Open Settings > Billing.
  2. Select Cancel subscription.
  3. Confirm the cancellation in the dialog.
